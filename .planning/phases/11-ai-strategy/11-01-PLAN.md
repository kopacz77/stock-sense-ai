<objective>
Encode the operator's article-intake thrift decision (CONTEXT.md "Article intake thrift", 2026-08-27) as a pure, synchronous, pre-LLM ranking function that decides *what gets scored first* under the existing 500/day soft cap — and prove, offline against the real 2026 corpus, that scoring only the top half of the ranked stream still catches >= 85% of the high-materiality articles.

Purpose: only 7% of the 18,139 LLM-scored articles are high-materiality, and 43% of intake matches no ticker and no topic keyword for a 4% hit rate. The current `isPriorityArticle()` hook is a coarse boolean (watchlist ticker OR any macro keyword) that cannot express "US company first, China second, war-zone third". This plan replaces the boolean with a ranked score so the same LLM budget buys materially more signal.

Output: `materiality-prescreen.ts` (pure ranking module), a committed held-out-week fixture, a retention test that fails if the >= 85% bar regresses, an `intel prescreen-eval` command for re-measuring against live data, and the `cycle-runner.ts` sort-key swap.

This plan is independent of `src/strategy/` and runs in Wave 1 alongside the tracer plan (11-02).
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-11-01-01 | Tampering | `predictMateriality` keyword matching | low | mitigate | Headline/summary are matched with `String.prototype.includes` on a lower-cased copy only — no `RegExp` built from article text (no ReDoS surface), no `eval`, no shell interpolation |
