<objective>
Add the two data-constrained signal types on the terms CONTEXT sets for them: SENTIMENT_VELOCITY gated on real scored-article coverage, FADE_OVERSHOOT shadow-only. Neither is allowed to quietly degrade into a zero — a missing-data day must announce itself, and a counter-trend idea must be logged without being tradeable.

Purpose: `scored-articles-*.jsonl` covers 36 non-contiguous days ending 2026-07-26 and nothing from 07-27 → 08-27 until the backlog drain runs; every `ticker-day-summary` row in that window has `articleCount: 0`. A naive 3-day sentiment delta over that window reads as "sentiment didn't move" when the truth is "we never looked". The gate is what makes that distinction visible, and shadow mode is what lets the operator accumulate FADE evidence for v2 without v1 taking the risk.

Output: `coverage.ts` (the gate primitive), `SentimentVelocityModule`, `FadeOvershootModule`, each fully unit-tested including against the real outage window.

Runs in Wave 2 in parallel with 11-03; the two plans share no files.
</objective>
