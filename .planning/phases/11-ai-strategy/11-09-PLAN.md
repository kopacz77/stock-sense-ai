<objective>
Close the operator's after-tax / after-fees gap: make every ranked candidate's target prove it can clear real round-trip costs and real tax before it is shown as tradeable, under whichever of two fully-specified jurisdiction rule sets the operator is currently living in.

Purpose: the phase shipped an engine that suggests entry/target/stop with no notion of what a trade actually costs to open, close and pay tax on. A 1.5:1 gross reward:risk that becomes 1.0:1 after Ontario capital-gains tax and two spread legs is not a 1.5:1 trade, and the operator was being shown it as one. The operator lives in Ontario now and will move to California, so this cannot be one rate knob with a label — both rule sets are identified completely, operator-editable, and every number is annotated with where it came from and a standing instruction to confirm it with an accountant.

Output: a `config/tax-profiles.json` carrying both jurisdictions, a `src/strategy/costs.ts` that turns a profile plus the operator's own numbers into a two-part hurdle, an engine that demotes candidates failing it (never re-targets them), a `strategy costs --show` sanity-check command, a wash-sale / superficial-loss flag read off the decision log, `afterTaxRewardUsd` on accepted decisions, gross-and-after-cost backtest reporting, and the net R:R on the web card.

Runs as Wave 6, alone, after the phase's own verification. Autonomous: the safety design carries the risk that would otherwise need a human gate — `costs.marginalRatePct` ships as `null`, so out of the box the engine applies NO tax haircut, guesses NO bracket, and demotes nothing on an unconfirmed tax number. The operator opts into the tax leg by entering their own rate after reading `strategy costs --show`.
