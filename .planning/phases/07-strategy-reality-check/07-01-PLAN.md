<objective>
Add an arbitrary date-range fetch method to `YahooFinanceProvider` and wire it into `MarketDataService.fetchHistoricalData()` as the final fallback after Alpha Vantage and Finnhub. This unblocks fetching 8 years of daily bars for 35 tickers without exhausting Alpha Vantage's 25-req/day free-tier quota.

Purpose: Without this, the M2-01 universe prefetch (Plan 03) would take ≥2 calendar days on Alpha Vantage's free tier alone, and Finnhub's `/stock/candle` endpoint is widely reported as premium-only for US equities. Yahoo is free, unlimited, and supports the full 2018-2025 range in a single call per symbol.

Output: Yahoo provider now exposes `fetchHistoricalDataRange(symbol, from, to)`; MarketDataService's enhanced `fetchHistoricalData` path falls back to Yahoo and writes results to the cache.
