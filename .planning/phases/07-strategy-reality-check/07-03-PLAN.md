<objective>
Prefetch daily OHLCV bars for the locked 35-ticker M2-01 universe (30 single names + 5 ETFs from `07-CONTEXT.md`) across 2018-01-01 → 2025-12-31, leveraging the Yahoo fallback added in Plan 01. Produce a `07-03-prefetch-report.md` documenting per-ticker bar counts, gaps, and data-quality flags.

Purpose: With ~1,050 backtests planned in Plan 05 (35 tickers × 2 strategies × ~15 grid points), reading from network on every run is infeasible. Prefetching once means Plan 05 runs are I/O-free and resumable. The prefetch report gives Plan 06 the data-quality context to flag any "results may be degraded by data gaps" cases in `RECOMMENDATION.md`.

Output: All 35 tickers cached on disk in `data/cache/historical/`; prefetch report at `.planning/phases/07-strategy-reality-check/07-03-prefetch-report.md`.
